Delete your data

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Last updated: October 20, 2026 · App: ADHD Focus Companion (package com.chartmann.adhdfocuscompanion) · Developer: Hartmann Studios

ADHD Focus keeps your data on your phone. Here is how to delete it, and what happens to purchases.

Delete inside the app

Swipe or long-press a task or a session in History to delete it. Medication reminders are deleted from the reminders list.

Focus rooms

Leave the room (or turn off the focus-rooms opt-in in Settings) and your presence entry is removed from the room service.

Delete everything

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → ADHD Focus → Storage → Clear storage. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing on our side to delete.

Pro subscription

The subscription is tied to your Google Play account, not to the app's data. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. After reinstalling, Settings → Restore purchases brings Pro back while the subscription is active.

Data held by Google

Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing is retained by Google under its own policies; you can manage ad settings in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads and at My Ad Center.

Need help?

open an issue on GitHub with "Privacy or data question" and we will help. Please do not post personal data in the issue.
